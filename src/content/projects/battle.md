## The challenge

Battle Cap is a one-to-four-player party naval shooter with competitive and cooperative modes, developed by a team at MechAlmond Productions.

## My contribution

Contributed as a technical art intern, working on the implementation and adjustment of art effects. The broader internship work involved scene rendering, 3D effects, visual animation and character actions.

## Approach

The work connected visual direction with practical implementation, translating stylized art requirements into effects that could function within the game production pipeline.

## Outcome

Battle Cap is a released team project. It received the Silver Award in the industry track of Tencent’s 2024 Global Game Competition, as noted in the supplied CV.
